# Fees, Scholarships and Financial Aid

## Fee payment and deadlines

Semester fees are due by [[deadline]]. Students with pending fees may be unable to register for examinations or access results until the dues are cleared, so contact the Accounts Section early if you expect a delay — options are available before the deadline that may not be available after it.

## Instalment plans

Students facing temporary financial difficulty can request to pay the semester fee in instalments. Submit the instalment request form to the Accounts Section ([[room / email]]) with a short explanation. Requests are usually decided within [[n]] working days.

## Scholarships and fee reimbursement

- Government scholarships: eligible students can apply through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) and applicable state schemes (for example Telangana ePASS for eligible students in Telangana). Check eligibility and deadlines on the official portals.
- Institutional merit and need-based scholarships: applications open [[month]] each year through the Scholarship Cell.
- Emergency financial assistance: the Student Welfare Fund can provide short-term support for students facing a sudden financial crisis (for example loss of family income or a medical emergency). Apply through the Dean of Student Affairs.

## Education loans

The Accounts Section can issue the fee structure and bonafide certificate needed for an education loan application.

Accounts Section: [[room / phone / email]]. Scholarship Cell: [[room / email]]. Requests are handled confidentially.
