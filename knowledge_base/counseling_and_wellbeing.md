# Counseling and Mental Health Support

## Campus Counseling Centre

The Counseling Centre offers free, confidential one-to-one sessions with qualified counselors for stress, anxiety, low mood, homesickness, relationship difficulties, exam pressure and any other personal concern. Location: [[building / room]]. Hours: [[hours]]. Book via [[email / phone / portal]]. Walk-ins are welcome for urgent concerns, and same-day appointments are kept free for students in distress.

Confidentiality: what you share stays with the counselor, except where there is a serious risk to your safety or someone else's.

## Crisis and emergency support

If you are thinking about harming yourself, feel unsafe, or are in crisis:

- Tele-MANAS, the national mental health helpline: call 14416 or 1-800-891-4416 (free, 24x7, multiple languages).
- Emergency services: 112.
- Campus emergency contact / security: [[number]].
- Go to the Counseling Centre or tell any faculty member or warden — they will help you get support immediately.

## Faculty mentors

Every student is assigned a faculty mentor. Mentors are a first point of contact for academic or personal difficulties and can connect you with the right office.
