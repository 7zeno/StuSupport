# Academic Support Services

## Tutoring and remedial classes

Departments run free remedial classes for core courses with high failure rates, and a peer tutoring programme where senior students help juniors. Sign up through the department office or [[portal link]].

## Study skills and time management

The Learning Centre offers short workshops on time management, exam preparation and note-taking, and one-to-one study-skills consultations. Students who are working part-time or commuting long distances can ask for help building a realistic weekly schedule.

## Re-planning your course load

If you are struggling to keep up, your faculty mentor can help you decide which courses to prioritise, whether to clear backlogs before taking new electives, and how to use supplementary examinations.

## Library and digital resources

The library provides textbooks on loan, previous question papers, and access to online learning platforms. [[hours / link]]

## Students with disabilities or health conditions

Students with a documented disability or long-term health condition can request reasonable accommodations (for example extra exam time or a scribe) through the Equal Opportunity Cell. [[contact]]
