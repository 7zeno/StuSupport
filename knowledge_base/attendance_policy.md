# Attendance Policy

## Minimum attendance requirement

Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to write the end-semester examination for that course. Attendance is calculated from the start of the semester and is updated on the student portal every week.

## Condonation for attendance shortage

Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation on medical or other genuine grounds. The application must be submitted to the Head of Department with supporting documents (for example a medical certificate) before the examination registration deadline, along with the condonation fee of [[amount]]. Condonation can be granted a limited number of times during the program.

Students below 65% attendance are not eligible for condonation and must re-register for the course in a later semester.

## If your attendance is falling

- Check your course-wise attendance on the student portal every week, not just at the end of the semester.
- Talk to your faculty mentor early. Mentors can help plan how to recover attendance and can flag genuine difficulties (health, family, travel, finances) to the department.
- If you missed classes because of illness, keep medical records; they are needed for condonation.
