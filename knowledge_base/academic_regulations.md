# Academic Regulations: Backlogs, Supplementary Exams and Academic Standing

## Backlogs

A backlog is a course that a student has registered for but not yet passed. Backlogs can be cleared by appearing in the supplementary examination or the next regular examination for that course. There is no limit on the number of attempts within the maximum program duration, but each attempt requires examination registration and fee payment.

## Supplementary examinations

Supplementary examinations are held [[timing, e.g. within six weeks of results]]. Registration opens on the examination portal after results are declared. Missing the registration window means waiting for the next regular examination.

## Promotion and detention rules

To be promoted to the next year, a student must earn the minimum number of credits specified in the program regulations ([[credit requirement]]). Students who do not meet the credit requirement are detained and may continue after clearing enough backlogs.

## Academic probation and recovery

Students whose CGPA falls below [[threshold, e.g. 5.0]] or who accumulate several backlogs are placed on academic watch. This is not a punishment; it means the student is assigned a faculty mentor and is offered tutoring support to recover.

## Maximum program duration

Students must complete the program within [[e.g. twice the normal duration]] years. Students close to this limit should meet the Controller of Examinations office to plan the remaining courses.

## Improving CGPA

- Prioritise clearing backlogs first: passing a course raises earned credits and CGPA together.
- Use the free tutoring and peer-mentoring sessions (see Academic Support Services).
- Meet your faculty mentor to plan a realistic course load for the next semester.
